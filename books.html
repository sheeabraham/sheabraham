One book a week

I am on a reading challenge to read one book a week for a year. Here are the books classified by month, along with a brief review.

April

A young girl begins an adulterous relationship with a married police officer knowing well that it is not likely to last and that it is deceitful. Despite these misgivings, she falls in love with the man. How these actions affect her and those around her is the crux of the story. A retired actress trying to get back into the film industry and an issue of land grabbing are sub plots.
